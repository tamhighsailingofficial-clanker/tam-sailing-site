Practice

Expectations

Sailors are expected to attend the majority of practices and with an enthusiasm to learn and sail.

Upon arrival, sailors should begin rigging their boats and getting dressed afterwards. In order to maximize our limited time on water, this should be done as fast as possible.

Required Gear

  • Closed-toed shoes
  • USCG-approved life jacket
  • Wetsuit and/or full spray gear
  • Helmet

Optional Gear

  • Water / snack
  • Sunscreen
  • Sunglasses
  • Neoprene boots
  • Towel
  • Change of clothes
  • Gloves
