Tam High Sailing

About Us

Tam High is part of the PCISA NorCal League and sails FJs (Flying Juniors) out of Sausalito Yacht Club. We practice with sailors from Marin Catholic and San Marin for a combined total of 24 participants in an average season.

To participate, sailors must pay a fee and register via Clubspot. Team members are selected by the coach. Prior sailing experience is highly recommended, but not required; however, due to facility limitations students with experience may be given priority.

Practices are Mondays & Wednesdays from 4:00-7:00pm in September and October. We compete against other PCISA schools at weekend regattas, which are optional but highly encouraged.
