FAQ

Where can I park?

There are two nearby paid parking lots, metered street parking, and limited free parkingat the Spinnaker. Please do not park in the SYC parking lot.

Do I need prior sailing experience to sign up?

Basic sailing skills are strongly recommended. Summer programs at SYC and other yacht clubs offer introductory FJ classes.

How can I / my child get involved?

Sign up via the clubspot link on the homepage (while registration is open) or feel free to reach out via our email/Instagram (found under "Contact Us")!

What gear should I bring to practice?

Required: closed-toed shoes, life jacket, wetsuit or full spray gear, helmet
Optional: gloves, sunglasses

Are sailors required to attend regattas?

Regattas are not mandatory, but sailors are expected to put effort into the team.

What is the best ice cream spot for after practice?

Lappert’s Ice Cream!!
